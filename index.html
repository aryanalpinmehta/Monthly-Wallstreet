How This Works

Methodology

Approach

Each report fixes the macro data, sector metrics, and technical picture as they stood on the publication date. Fundamentals anchor the thesis; technical signals confirm the entry. Hindsight stays out of the argument.

Accountability

Every report carries a specific 12-month price target and a retrospective verdict. The outcome column records what happened. No revisions, no retroactive caveats, no asterisks.

Scope

Coverage runs across equities, commodities, fixed income, real estate, and digital assets. Selection rule: the asset with the strongest risk-adjusted fundamental case, confirmed by a constructive technical setup.
